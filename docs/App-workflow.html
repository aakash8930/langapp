Part Three · Beyond the Baseline

Where it goes past Duolingo and Busuu

Everything above is table stakes. This is the AI layer that turns a lesson app into a tutor — and the wedge is instant correction: Busuu's best feature depends on a native speaker showing up, while this answers every time, at any hour. It lives in the shared core, so every language track inherits it for free.

壱 · 01

A patient partner that speaks, listens, and holds a real conversation at the learner's level.
弐 · 02
Real-time pronunciation scoring
Every spoken phrase graded on the spot, down to the specific sounds worth repeating.
参 · 03
Handwriting recognition for kana & kanji
Draw the character; the app checks shape and stroke order, not just the final answer.
四 · 04
Personalized learning paths
The route bends around each learner's pace, goals, and the things they keep getting wrong.
五 · 05
Interactive stories & role-play
Practice inside scenes — ordering food, asking directions — instead of isolated drills.
六 · 06
Native-level grammar explanations
Ask why a sentence works, and get an answer with the nuance a textbook skips.
七 · 07
JLPT prep with adaptive AI coaching
Tier-by-tier study that shifts focus toward weak spots as the exam gets closer.
八 · 08
Analytics & tailored spaced repetition
Review timing tuned per learner, so words resurface exactly when they're about to fade.

Part Six · Build Phasing

The order to build it

The catalog above is years of work. This is a sequence, not a to-do list — ship a thin, complete flagship first, then earn each expansion.

The rule: every phase is independently shippable and useful on its own. Nothing later blocks a learner from getting value now. A few catalog items — offline on-device AI, OCR, the browser extension, and a payments-and-moderation marketplace — are near-separate products; they sit last on purpose, alongside the Part Seven institutional systems (teacher portal, agent ecosystem, cultural hub). One exception worth pulling forward: build a thin Knowledge Graph early, because SRS and lesson recommendation lean on it from Phase 1 on — you don't need the full graph, just the skeleton.

Never interrupt learning to sell

The one promise that sits above every feature on this map. Busuu's failing isn't that it has a premium tier — it's that the upsell lands mid-lesson, and the ads break the one thing a learner came for. That frustration is shared by millions, and it's a position neither incumbent can easily take.
